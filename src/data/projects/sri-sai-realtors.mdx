## Overview

Sri Sai Realtors is an interior fit-out company established in 2018, delivering end-to-end solutions across commercial, corporate, retail, aviation, warehousing and large-scale infrastructure environments. With in-house furniture manufacturing and a single-framework project management approach, they act as one accountable partner from planning to handover.

I built their corporate website to communicate this integrated execution model, build trust with enterprise clients, and drive project enquiries.

## The Goal

- Present Sri Sai as a credible, pan-India fit-out partner for large commercial clients
- Showcase the full range of capabilities in one place: interiors, furniture, civil & MEP, specialized projects and maintenance
- Highlight aviation credibility, including BCAS security clearance for controlled airport environments
- Convert visitors into enquiries via contact form, phone and WhatsApp

## Key Sections

### Capabilities
Five core service areas: Commercial Interior Fit-Out, Manufacturing Furniture, Civil & MEP, Specialized Projects (airports, warehouses, fulfilment centres) and MAC Maintenance (renovation, alteration, refurbishment).

### Selected Work
A curated work section featuring aviation projects (Air India, GMR Immigration Counters at IGI Airport, IndiGo, American Airlines) and dark store projects (Blinkit, Zepto).

### Industries We Serve
Corporate, Retail, Aviation, Warehousing, Fulfilment Centres, Hospitality, Museums and Infrastructure.

### In-House Manufacturing ("Our Unit")
A dedicated section on their own furniture facility: workstations, executive furniture, conference tables, reception counters, storage systems, pedestals and retail furniture, with customization and quality inspection.

### Aviation Experience
Explains their capability in controlled airport environments: BCAS clearance (5-year validity), terminal works (landside & airside), restricted-hour execution and protocol adherence.

### From Plan to Handover
A seven-stage process visualised step by step: Plan, Procure, Manufacture, Execute, Inspect, Install, Handover.

### Client Trust
An infinite logo marquee showing clients across sectors, including Air India, IndiGo, Coca-Cola, Microsoft, McDonald's, Flipkart, Blinkit and Zepto.

## Tech & Implementation

- **Next.js + TypeScript** for a fast, SEO-friendly, type-safe multi-page site (Home, About, Work, Why Sri Sai, Our Unit, Contact)
- **Tailwind CSS** for a clean, responsive and consistent design system
- **Framer Motion** for scroll-based reveals, the process timeline and the client logo marquee
- **Cloudflare** for DNS, CDN and performance/security
- **SEO**: structured meta tags, Open Graph and Twitter cards, and keyword-targeted metadata for fit-out and furniture search terms
- **Lead capture**: sticky call and WhatsApp CTAs across the site

## Outcome

A polished, fast and mobile-friendly website that positions Sri Sai Realtors as an integrated, enterprise-ready fit-out partner and gives prospective clients clear, low-friction ways to get in touch.
