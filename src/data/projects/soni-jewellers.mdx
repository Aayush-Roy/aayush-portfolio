## Overview

**Soni Jewellers** is a premium jewellery website built around the brand's heritage, craftsmanship, and trust. The website combines a luxury-focused visual experience with sections showcasing jewellery collections, gold and silver products, craftsmanship, brand history, and customer consultation.

The brand highlights a legacy spanning generations, with a focus on certified jewellery, hallmarking, craftsmanship, and contemporary designs.

## Key Features

* **Premium Luxury UI**: Designed a sophisticated visual experience that reflects the premium nature of the jewellery brand.
* **Heritage Storytelling**: Created an immersive brand story highlighting Soni Jewellers' history, craftsmanship, and multi-generation legacy.
* **Jewellery Collections**: Added curated sections for rings, necklaces, earrings, and other jewellery categories with interactive product showcases.
* **Gold & Silver Products**: Dedicated sections for gold coins, silver coins, gold bars, and silver cast bars.
* **Certification & Purity**: Highlighted BIS hallmarking, HUID certification, 999.9 purity, and product authenticity information.
* **Interactive Gallery**: Built an immersive gallery experience for showcasing jewellery collections and handcrafted pieces.
* **Craftsmanship Showcase**: Presented the brand's materials, master artisans, heritage techniques, and quality assurance process.
* **Smooth Animations**: Used GSAP and Framer Motion to create polished transitions, scroll animations, and interactive UI elements.
* **Consultation Flow**: Added a contact and private consultation section allowing customers to enquire about jewellery and book an appointment.
* **Responsive Experience**: Optimized the interface across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
* **Production Deployment**: Deployed the website on Hostinger with a production-ready setup.

## Tech Stack

* **Next.js** — Application framework and frontend architecture
* **TypeScript** — Type-safe development
* **Tailwind CSS** — Responsive styling and UI development
* **Framer Motion** — UI transitions and interactive animations
* **GSAP** — Advanced scroll-based and cinematic animations
* **Hostinger** — Production hosting and deployment

## Impact

The website transforms Soni Jewellers' traditional jewellery identity into a modern digital experience, combining **heritage, craftsmanship, luxury, and interactive storytelling**.

The platform gives visitors a digital way to explore jewellery collections, discover the brand's legacy, learn about product authenticity and craftsmanship, and connect with the jewellery store for consultations.
