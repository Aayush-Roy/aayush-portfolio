# Arotahi Tech

Built the corporate website for Arotahi, a Wellington-based technology and business services company (a subsidiary of Entelect). Arotahi combines established BPO capabilities with Agentic AI, automation, and human-like voice customer engagement to help businesses operate smarter and improve customer experience.

The site was built with React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS for a fast, responsive layout, with Framer Motion used for smooth section and interaction animations, and deployed on Cloudflare for global, low-latency delivery.

The goal was to clearly communicate Arotahi's "Human + AI" operating model — where AI handles speed, scale, and repetitive interactions while human teams handle judgement, empathy, and relationship management — across their core service areas: customer experience, sales operations, workflow automation, and digital growth.
