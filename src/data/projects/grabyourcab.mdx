## Overview

**GrabYourCab** is a full-service cab and car rental platform serving **Delhi NCR, Goa, and Noida International Airport (Jewar)**. The platform allows customers to explore and book different types of transportation services, including city and outstation taxis, self-drive cars, luxury vehicles, tempo travellers, luxury buses, corporate transportation, and customized travel services.

The platform was designed to provide a smooth and modern booking experience while making it easy for users to discover vehicles, compare services, explore locations, and initiate bookings through WhatsApp.

## Key Features

* **Cab & Taxi Services**: Supports city, local, and outstation cab bookings with 24/7 availability.
* **Self-Drive Rentals**: Users can explore vehicles available for hourly, daily, and weekly self-drive rentals.
* **Luxury Car Rentals**: Premium vehicles including Audi, Mercedes, and BMW are available for events, weddings, and corporate travel.
* **Fleet Browsing**: Built a vehicle browsing experience with categories such as economical cars, SUVs/MUVs, and luxury cars.
* **Multiple Service Categories**: Includes taxi services, self-drive rentals, luxury cars, tempo travellers, luxury buses, customized trips, and corporate transportation.
* **Location-Based Services**: Platform serves Delhi NCR, Goa, and Jewar Airport with dedicated service sections.
* **Airport Transfers**: Provides fixed-fare transfers to and from Noida International Airport (Jewar), along with Delhi airport routes.
* **WhatsApp Booking Flow**: Customers can select a service and vehicle and proceed with booking confirmation through WhatsApp.
* **Corporate Travel**: Supports employee transportation, airport transfers, monthly billing, GST invoices, and dedicated corporate travel services.
* **Responsive UI**: Designed the experience to work smoothly across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

## Tech Stack

* **Next.js** — Application framework and frontend architecture
* **TypeScript** — Type-safe development
* **Tailwind CSS** — Responsive UI styling
* **PostgreSQL** — Relational data storage
* **Supabase** — Backend infrastructure and database integration

## Impact

The platform brings multiple transportation services under a single digital experience, allowing customers to discover vehicles, explore service locations, check available rental options, and initiate bookings quickly.

GrabYourCab currently operates across **Delhi NCR, Goa, and Jewar Airport**, with a fleet spanning from economical hatchbacks to luxury vehicles and larger group-transport options.
