## Overview

The Laziz Tiffin Service is a small tiffin business that cooks fresh, everyday home-style meals: pure veg lunch, dinner and light breakfast. They are not a cloud kitchen or an aggregator, and they wanted a website that reflects that: simple, warm and personal.

I built the site to help customers discover the meal options quickly and reach the kitchen directly.

## The Goal

- Communicate the "ghar jaisa khana" feel through warm visuals and simple copy
- Showcase the three meal categories: Daily Lunch, Dinner, and Breakfast & Light Meals
- Remove friction: no app, no checkout, no subscription flow, just call or WhatsApp
- Rank for local searches like tiffin service, daily tiffin, lunch tiffin and dinner tiffin

## Key Sections

### Hero
A bold "Ghar Jaisa Khana. Har Roz." headline with primary CTAs: **Find Your Tiffin** and **Call Us**.

### About
Explains the home-style, small-batch approach, with highlights like home-style preparation, convenient daily meals and direct communication with the kitchen.

### Our Tiffin
Cards for each meal option (Daily Lunch as "Most Popular", Dinner, Breakfast & Light Meals) with Pure Veg tags and per-meal **WhatsApp enquiry buttons** that open WhatsApp with a pre-filled message for that exact meal.

### Why Choose Us
Four reasons: comforting home-style food, convenient daily enquiries, direct communication with the kitchen, and flexibility around preferences.

### How It Works
A 3-step flow: explore the meal options, call or message, and arrange your daily tiffin.

### Contact & Location
Call, WhatsApp and Google Maps directions in one place.

## Tech & Implementation

- **Next.js + TypeScript** for a fast, SEO-friendly multi-page site (home and a dedicated Find Tiffin page)
- **Tailwind CSS** for a clean, responsive, mobile-first layout
- **Framer Motion** for smooth scroll reveals and card animations
- **WhatsApp deep links** with pre-filled, meal-specific messages to turn browsing into enquiries in one tap
- **SEO**: meta tags, Open Graph / Twitter cards and `en_IN` locale for local discovery

## Outcome

A lightweight, mobile-friendly website that lets customers go from "what's on the menu?" to chatting with the kitchen in a couple of taps, while keeping the personal, home-style feel of the business.
