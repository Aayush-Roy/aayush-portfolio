# Entelect

Built a modern corporate website for Entelect, showcasing its business process outsourcing (BPO), customer experience, and AI-powered business solutions. The platform communicates how businesses can combine human expertise with intelligent automation to streamline operations, improve customer engagement, and drive sales growth.

The website was developed using React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS to deliver a responsive, modern, and performant user experience. Framer Motion was used to create smooth transitions and engaging interface animations, while Cloudflare supports deployment and content delivery.

The goal was to present Entelect's capabilities across customer support, sales outsourcing, lead generation, contact center operations, and AI-driven automation. The website highlights the integration of Generative AI, chatbots, voice bots, and intelligent workflows to help businesses scale operations, improve customer interactions, and optimize business processes.
